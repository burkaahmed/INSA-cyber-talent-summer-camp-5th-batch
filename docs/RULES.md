# Camp Rules

## Authorized Scope

- Work only on challenge hosts and networks designated by camp instructors.
- Do not scan networks outside the lab or test real-world third-party services.
- Use unique accounts and avoid destructive actions.

## Team Behavior

- Collaborate and share knowledge with teammates.
- Do not publish challenge secrets or flags before the event ends.
- Credit sources and teammates in solution reports.
- Do not impersonate staff or competitors.

## Scoring

- A valid flag earns the points assigned to a challenge.
- Partial credit may be awarded for documented progress.
- Sportsmanship is required; penalties may apply for rule violations.

## Safety

Stop immediately and notify an instructor if a challenge exposes unexpected real-world data or external credentials.
